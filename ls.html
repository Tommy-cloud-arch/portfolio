Learning Support Philosophy

That intersection — inside the classroom and beyond — is what I hope to build, together.
My Approach

How I Support Learners

01
Design for Access
I apply Universal Design for Learning (UDL) principles to ensure that instructional materials, tasks, and assessments are accessible to all learners before barriers arise — not as an afterthought.
02
Collaborate to Include
Effective inclusion happens through teacher partnership. I work alongside subject teachers to co-plan lessons, adapt curriculum, modify assessments, and deliver in-class support through co-teaching.
03
Personalise with Purpose
I develop and implement Individual Learning Plans (ILPs) aligned with classroom objectives, monitor student progress through formative assessment, and adjust strategies to meet each learner's evolving needs.
In Practice

What I Actually Do in School

Co-Teaching & In-Class Support
Working Alongside Subject Teachers
I work alongside subject teachers in secondary classrooms, co-teaching to deliver differentiated instruction in real time. This includes adapting tasks on the spot, scaffolding complex texts, and supporting students to engage with the curriculum at their own access point.
Collaborative Planning
Designing Inclusive Units Together
I work with classroom teachers during unit planning to embed differentiation and access strategies from the outset — modifying assessments, creating tiered tasks, and aligning support with broader skill development goals.
ILP Development & Monitoring
Individualised Support that Connects to Class
I develop Individual Learning Plans grounded in classroom objectives, not isolated from them. Plans are communicated to all subject teachers and reviewed regularly, ensuring support is consistent, purposeful, and evidence-informed.
Assessment Accommodation
Ensuring Fair Access to Assessment
I coordinate assessment accommodations across all subjects — including extended time, modified format, and assistive technology provision — ensuring students can demonstrate their understanding without being disadvantaged by their learning barriers.
Family & Stakeholder Communication
Building a Shared Picture of the Learner
I maintain regular communication with families, external specialists, and school counsellors to build a complete and shared understanding of each learner — ensuring that support is coherent across school and home contexts.
Teacher Capacity Building
Supporting the Adults Around the Learner
I see teacher development as core to my role. I share strategies, model inclusive practices, and build shared language around differentiation and access — so that inclusive practice becomes embedded in school culture, not dependent on a single specialist.

Inclusive Practice

How I Embed Inclusion in School

Learning Skills Support
I embed learning skills development into support plans — helping students build the self-management, communication, and thinking skills they need to thrive independently.
Collaborative Unit Planning
I collaborate with teachers during unit planning to ensure differentiation is built in from the start — adapting assessment criteria, scaffolding inquiry tasks, and ensuring access to core concepts.
Assessment Access & Inclusion
I coordinate assessment access arrangements across subjects and support students to manage the academic demands of secondary school alongside their individual learning needs.
Whole-Learner Approach
I design support that nurtures the whole learner — helping students become more reflective, open-minded, and resilient through structured, scaffolded experiences.
Inquiry & Access
I adapt inquiry-based tasks to ensure students with diverse needs can access and contribute — through modified entry points, visual scaffolds, and tiered questioning.
Whole-School Inclusion
I work beyond the individual to build inclusive school culture — sharing strategies school-wide, building shared language around inclusion, and supporting all teachers to feel confident and equipped.
